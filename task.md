This is the official website of Lazy Alien Server, which is short for 'LAS', a Chinese Minecraft Server Organization.

## First Step

## Framework

Use **Vue** or **React** for framework, **scss** for stylesheet, **typescript** for front-end script. Properly use global.css, variables, components and layout and make them reusable.
